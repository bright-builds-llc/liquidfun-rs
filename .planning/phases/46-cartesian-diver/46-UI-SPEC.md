## Canvas Drawing Contract

Everything the visitor sees besides water comes from `collect_segments` (bottle) and `collect_circles` (diver, bubble). The physical static walls are thick polygons and are **not** drawn directly; the segments are their drawn outline. One ramped squeeze value `s ∈ [0, 1]` drives density, dent and bubble together (D-06, D-09, D-10).

### Nominal geometry (meters)

**[UI-D60]** Nominal values, chosen within D-11 for framing. The executor may retune physics constants inside D-04..D-11, but whenever a geometry constant changes, `CARTESIAN_DIVER_VIEW_BOUNDS`, the hit region and the `portrait-bounds.test.ts` wall points are updated **in the same change** (Phase 31 rule).

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| Particle radius | 0.05 |
| Bottle interior (inner faces of the physical walls) | x ∈ [−1.2, 1.2], y ∈ [0, 5.6] (2.4 m by 5.6 m) |
| Physical wall thickness | 0.4 (outer faces at x = ±1.6, floor bottom y = −0.4, cap top y = 6.0); overlapping static polygons, closed cap |
| Water | fills the interior to about y = 5.3 (small headspace under the cap), at most about 3,000 particles |
| Diver | one dynamic circle, radius R = 0.6 (at least about 6 particle diameters, at most 25% of the interior width so water passes it); center in the top third (y ≥ 3.73) at load and after a rise |
| Drawn outline offset G | 0.25 outside the inner faces: rectangle x = ±1.45, y = −0.25 to 5.85 |
| Dent depth D | 0.25 (= G) |
| Squeeze hit region | physical outer rectangle plus 0.3: x ∈ [−1.9, 1.9], y ∈ [−0.7, 6.3] (D-02) |

### Bottle outline and dent (`collect_segments`)

**[UI-D61]** The outline sits G outside the water-facing inner faces, and the dent depth equals G. At full squeeze the bowed line just meets the inner face at mid-height and **never crosses into the water**, so particles never appear outside the drawn bottle. At rest the gap reads as the bottle's wall thickness.

- Always return exactly **14 segments**, in this order (a closed loop, deterministic): floor (−1.45, −0.25) → (1.45, −0.25); right wall, 6 segments, bottom to top; cap (1.45, 5.85) → (−1.45, 5.85); left wall, 6 segments, top to bottom.
- Side-wall points: for `k = 0..=6`, `t = k / 6`, `y = −G + t · (H + 2G)` with `H = 5.6`, inward offset `d(t) = D · s · sin(TAU / 2 · t)` (tau-based per repository convention). Right wall `x = 1.45 − d(t)`; left wall `x = −1.45 + d(t)`. Endpoints (`t = 0, 1`) have zero offset and meet the floor and cap corners.
- At `s = 0` the six side segments per wall are collinear; the count never changes, so the static segment layer repaints only while `s` is changing (`sameContent` in `static-layer.ts`).
- The physical walls never move (D-09); no fixture or body changes with `s`.

### Diver and bubble (`collect_circles`)

- Return exactly **two** circles, in order: `[ (diver_center, R), (bubble_center, r_b) ]`. No labels.
- Bubble radius `r_b = R · (0.6 − 0.3 · s)` (0.6R released, 0.3R squeezed; D-10).
- **[UI-D62]** Bubble position: the bubble rides at the top of the diver, opposite gravity, so it shrinks toward the top as water "enters" from below. `bubble_center = diver_center + u · (0.92 · R − r_b)`, where `u = −world.gravity() / |world.gravity()|`, or `(0, 1)` when `|gravity| < 1e-6`. This follows the gravity slider and phone tilt. The bubble has no fixture and no physics.

### Squeeze value and timing (visual side of D-06, D-07)

| Concern | Contract |
|---------|----------|
| Ramp | `s` moves linearly toward its target (1 squeezed, 0 released) in **0.25 s** of simulation time, updated once per fixed step in `on_advance` (tunable within 0.2–0.3 s). The density, dent and bubble all read the same `s` |
| Opening frame | diver floating in the top third, `s = 0`: straight bottle sides, bubble at 0.6R. Nothing moves visibly until a press (water settles only); this is the SCENE-02 diver exception, and the hint invites the press |
| Sink | at gravity 10, the diver center reaches the bottom third (y < 1.87) within about 3 s of squeeze; the dent and small bubble stay visible for the whole hold |
| Rise | after release, the center returns to the top third within about 4 s; the dent unbows and the bubble regrows over 0.25 s |
| Motion feel | calm: moderate linear damping, no bounce off the floor or cap that reads as a collision glitch |

## Interaction Contract

| Input | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| Pointer `Down` inside the hit region | `squeezed = true` |
| Pointer `Down` outside the hit region | nothing (the hint says so) |
| Pointer `Move` | ignored |
| Pointer `Up` or `Cancel`, anywhere | `squeezed = false` (the shared canvas pointer code already captures the pointer and maps `pointercancel` / `lostpointercapture` to Cancel, so dragging off the canvas releases) |
| `Squeeze` button (click, Enter, Space) | flips `squeezed`; the last input wins (D-01) |
| Reset scene, Gravity change (recreates) | rebuilds with `squeezed = false`, `s = 0` |
| Paused | pointer and Squeeze still record intent; nothing moves until Play, then `on_advance` applies it |
| Cursor | the shared `canvas-interactive` crosshair, as for every pointer scene; no new cursor |

No physics runs in `apply_pointer` or `apply_action` (D-01). No HUD control, no hold infrastructure, no new control kind (D-03). The `Squeeze` button carries no `aria-pressed`: action controls are stateless in the shared component, and adding state would be a new control kind. **[UI-D63]** The squeeze state is conveyed visually (dent, bubble, diver motion) and by the hint's "use Squeeze again"; this limitation is accepted for this phase.
