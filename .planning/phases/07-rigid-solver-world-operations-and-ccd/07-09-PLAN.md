# Plan 09: TOI Islands, Sub-Stepping, and Work Budgets

## Objective

Solve accepted TOI events through bounded islands, integrate them into checked world stepping, and make partial continuous progress coherent and resumable.

## Decisions Covered

- D-13: accepted TOI events use the pinned bounded island construction and continuous solver behavior.
- D-14: stepping reports complete versus continuous-pending; sub-stepping accepts one TOI event and resumes without a public token.
- D-15: one aggregate work budget yields typed partial evidence while leaving the world coherent and resumable.

<threat_model>
- An oversized TOI island could overrun fixed solver storage or turn one hostile contact graph into unbounded work.
- Committing bodies before all constraints and proxy updates succeed could expose a half-solved continuous event.
- A budget stop at an arbitrary internal instruction could leave cache, sweep, contact, and proxy state mutually inconsistent.
- Re-running the discrete stage on resume could duplicate forces, integration, callbacks, or contact work.
</threat_model>
