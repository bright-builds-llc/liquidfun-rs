# Plan 07: Transactional Origin Shifting

## Objective

Implement checked, topology-preserving world-origin translation across body sweeps and broad-phase proxies, with query and ray-cast covariance tests.

## Decisions Covered

- D-20: origin shifting is a checked prepare/commit operation that subtracts the shift from world-space body, sweep, and tree AABB coordinates without changing topology.
- D-21: query, ray-cast, and origin-shift evidence covers covariance and unchanged identity/order constraints.

<threat_model>
- A non-finite shift or finite subtraction overflow could commit only part of the world and leave bodies and proxies in different frames.
- Rebuilding the tree during a shift could silently alter traversal order, proxy identity, or move-buffer state.
- Omitting sweep endpoints or fat AABBs could make later collision and CCD behavior depend on when the shift occurred.
- Shifting while the world is locked could invalidate solver-owned indices and staged state.
</threat_model>
