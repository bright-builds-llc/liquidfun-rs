# Plan 10: Phase 7 Protocol and Evidence Policy

## Objective

Define bounded, closed protocol additions and observable-specific evidence policies for Phase 7 before either implementation adapter emits them.

## Decisions Covered

- D-16: explicit anti-tunneling and continuous-progress witnesses demonstrate CCD behavior.
- D-19: query/ray canonicalization is evidence-only and distinguishes multisets, statuses, and minimum-fraction tie sets.
- D-21: query, ray-cast, and shift-origin witness families cover the locked edge cases.
- D-22: extend the existing rigid protocol and evidence workflow rather than create an unrestricted command language.
- D-23: structural fields are exact and every numeric observable has a closed, field-specific comparison policy.

<threat_model>
- Unbounded arrays, counts, strings, or nested actions could make the oracle and comparator vulnerable to resource exhaustion.
- A permissive catch-all action or result shape could hide schema drift between Rust and C++ adapters.
- Generic epsilon comparison could accept structurally wrong events or conceal material tunneling and solver errors.
- Evidence-only canonicalization could accidentally become a production ordering guarantee or erase duplicate occurrences.
</threat_model>
