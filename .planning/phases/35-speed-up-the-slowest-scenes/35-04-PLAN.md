<objective>
Build each chain child `EdgeShape` once per child per pass, not once per particle, in body-contact generation and in CCD ray casts. Keep the change only if the D-11 rule passes.

Purpose: PERF-08 for liquid-tumbler (D-02). Its glass is a chain. `ChainShape::child_edge` rebuilds and validates the edge for every particle query: about 4.7% self, 2.8% from `body_contact::generate` and 1.9% from CCD `ray_cast`. The hoist is bit-identical. `ChainShape::distance_to_point` and `ChainShape::ray_cast` are exactly `self.child_edge(child)?.<op>(...)` (chain.rs:157-180), so calling the op on a prebuilt edge runs the same float operations (D-07). Research prototype: liquid-tumbler about 22.1 → 21.2 ms/step, fingerprints identical.
Output: the engine change (kept or reverted) and its attempt row.
</objective>
