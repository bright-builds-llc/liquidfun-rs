# Plan 03-01: Typed Identity and Generational Arena Foundation

<objective>
Establish the safe identity substrate for later world objects: private process-unique world keys, distinct opaque typed handles, explicit lookup failures, and a custom arena that permanently retires an exhausted slot instead of allowing generation wrap.
</objective>

<threat_model>
- Forged handles are limited by private fields and constructors; tests inside the crate still exercise validation as though identities were corrupted.
- Stale and destroyed handles must not resolve after slot reuse, and handles from another world must produce a distinct cross-world error.
- Slot, world-key, and `u64` generation exhaustion must fail explicitly without wraparound, aliasing, or accidental reuse.
- A handle is an authority-free integer identity token: `Send + Sync` is allowed, but no unsafe manual auto-trait implementation may be added.
</threat_model>
