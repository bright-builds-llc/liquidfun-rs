## Canvas Drawing Contract

Everything the visitor sees besides water comes from `collect_segments`. `collect_circles` returns an empty list. The physical walls and vanes are thick polygons; their outlines are drawn, so **what is drawn is what collides** (SCENE-03).

### Nominal geometry (meters)

**[UI-D68]** Nominal values from the research geometry sketch, used here only for framing. The executor may retune physics constants inside D-04 and D-12, but whenever a geometry constant changes, the Rust `FRAME_*` constants, `ARCHIMEDES_SCREW_VIEW_BOUNDS` and the `portrait-bounds.test.ts` wall points are updated **in the same change** (Phase 31 rule).

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| Particle radius | 0.05 |
| Tilt | 30° (`TAU / 12`), axis `u = (0.8660254, 0.5)`, normal `n = (−0.5, 0.8660254)` as documented `f32` literals |
| Tube | interior gap 0.7, floor and roof slabs about 0.35 thick, length L = 5.0 between housing faces, origin O = (−4.0, −0.3) at the bottom housing face on the floor line |
| Housings | 1.0 long at each end, faces cut parallel to the vanes |
| Vanes | 0.3 thick, slanted 20° from `n` with the top leaning uphill, embedded 0.1 into the floor and roof, pitch 1.0 |
| Reservoir | trough from about x = −6.0 to the chute's outer wall, floor about y = −1.0 under the bottom housing, rising to the right under the tube |
| Basin and chute | basin under the outlet opening with its weir lip below the top housing's underside; a vertical chute outside the weir drops onto the high end of the reservoir floor |
| Water | at most about 3,000 particles; sealed pockets about half full at load; basin empty at load |

### Wall outlines

- Each static wall polygon (tube floor and roof slabs, both housings, reservoir walls and floor, basin walls and floor, weir, chute wall) is drawn as its closed polygon loop through a `push_loop` helper, the Hydraulic Fountain pattern. Seal overlaps between polygons are allowed to show; readability is checked in the README clip review.
- Wall segments are deterministic: the same order every frame, built once from geometry constants.

### Vanes

**[UI-D69]** Each vane is drawn as its parallelogram outline (4 segments) at its current transform, **clipped only against the two housing face lines**. The housing faces are parallel to the vanes, so the clip is a clamp in the vane-normal coordinate and keeps a parallelogram. A vane wholly inside a housing is omitted; a vane partly inside is cut at the face, so vanes appear to slide in and out of the housings and the one-pitch snap is invisible. The embedded tips (0.1 m into the floor and roof, and the bare tips at the inlet and outlet openings) are drawn as they collide, not clipped.

- Total segments stay far below the 512-segment frame cap (about 60 wall + at most 7 × 4 vane). No circles (cap 48 untouched).
- **[UI-D70]** No shaft line. Segments paint over the water, so a drawn axle through the pockets would read as a solid rod that water passes through. This keeps "drawn means solid" true. (D-14 left this to discretion.)
- The vane outlines change every frame while the screw moves, so the static segment layer repaints each frame, as it already does for Washing Machine and Hydraulic Fountain. At speed 0 the content is stable and the layer stops repainting.

### Motion and timing (visual side of D-09, D-10, D-17)

| Concern | Contract |
|---------|----------|
| Opening frame | reservoir filled, sealed pockets about half full, basin empty, vanes already moving forward at the default speed. The first pocket pours into the basin within about 2 s (D-09 requires within about 5 s at gravity 10) |
| Forward | separate pockets ride up between vanes and pour into the basin from the outlet opening; the basin rises to its lip and spills over the weir down the chute into the reservoir. The scene keeps running |
| Reverse | the vanes slide down; the outlet scoops water from the basin, pockets ride down and empty into the reservoir; the basin level falls below the lip and the chute stops |
| Speed change | live, no rebuild; takes effect on the next fixed step. Speed 0 holds the vanes still; the pockets stay sealed |
| Motion feel | steady and calm: no burst of particle speed at a snap, no water squirting from a housing, no particle visible inside a wall or vane |

## Interaction Contract

| Input | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| `Screw speed` spinner (drag, step buttons, arrow keys, Home/End through the shipped control) | sets rpm in `0..=60` in steps of 1; Rust returns `ControlEffect::Live` |
| `Direction` preset (`Forward` / `Reverse`) | sets the vane direction at the current speed; Rust returns `ControlEffect::Live` |
| Pointer on the canvas | nothing scene-specific: `apply_pointer` returns `Ok(())` (D-11). Dragging the screw is an anti-feature |
| Reset scene, Gravity change (recreates) | rebuilds the scene at load state. After a gravity rebuild the web replays the stored speed and direction (shipped `worker-controls.ts`). Reset scene follows the shipped player behavior for live controls, exactly as for Washing Machine's `Drum speed`; no scene-specific reset code |
| Paused | control changes record intent; nothing moves until Play, then `on_advance` applies it |
| Invalid control values | `Err(SessionError::UnknownControl)`; rpm accepts only canonical whole numbers `0..=max` (no leading zeros, signs or decimals); direction accepts exactly `forward` or `reverse` |
| Cursor | the shared canvas cursor for scenes without a scene pointer behavior, as for Washing Machine; no new cursor |

No physics runs in `apply_control`; it stores intent and `on_advance` applies motion once per fixed step (D-01, D-10).
