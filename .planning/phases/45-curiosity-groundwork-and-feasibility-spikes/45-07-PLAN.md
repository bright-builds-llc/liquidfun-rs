Metric definitions (fixed before measuring; sample every 15 steps = 0.25 s; gravity (0, -10); run 30 s = 1,800 steps; then flip and run 10 s more):
- `upper` = count(y > 0), `lower` = count(y < 0); flow = Δlower per sample.
- Jam (D-25): `upper > 0` and `lower` unchanged for more than 60 consecutive steps (checked every step, any time before the flip).
- Deposit = lower-bulb particles with y < -0.2 and speed < 0.5 m/s (excludes the falling stream).
- Bins: 15 bins of width 0.2 m across x -1.5..1.5; a bin is occupied with at least 3 deposit particles; bin top = max y of its deposit particles.
- `edge` = mean of the tops of the leftmost and rightmost occupied bins; `base_width` = right edge of the rightmost occupied bin − left edge of the leftmost occupied bin; `peak` = nearest-rank p95 of the occupied bin tops.
- `slope_deg = atan((peak − edge) / (base_width / 2))` in degrees (edge-relative reading of D-25's "p95 height against base width"; research Pitfall 3 / A4).
- Informational, also logged: `literal_d25_deg = atan((p95 of deposit particle y − (−3.4)) / (base_width / 2))` and `side_fit_deg` = mean |atan| of least-squares slopes of bin tops left and right of the highest bin.
- Pass (D-25): over the last 5 s (25-30 s, 21 samples) the median `m` of `slope_deg` is ≥ 20°, every sample lies in [m − 5°, m + 5°], no jam, AND `m` exceeds the water control's median by at least 15° (guard against a confinement artifact).
- Record per configuration: particles, mean native release ms/step (hook + step), slope samples, final `upper`/`lower`, jam yes/no with time, frozen count and freezes per step (candidate 2), max speed of frozen grains (candidate 2, assumption A3).
